systeme.io vs ClickFunnels for Beginners: An Honest Comparison

Prices, limits and the real trade-offs between systeme.io and ClickFunnels in 2026, and how to pick the right one for a business that's just starting.

October 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Disclosure: BizMasterPro is reader-supported. Some links are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission if you sign up for a paid plan, at no extra cost to you.

systeme.io and ClickFunnels both let you build sales funnels, send email, host courses and take payments in one place. The biggest difference for a beginner is price. ClickFunnels starts at $97 a month, while systeme.io has a free plan. But price isn't the only thing that matters, so here's a side-by-side look using each company's pricing page as of October 2026.

Disclosure: we're a systeme.io affiliate and may earn a commission if you upgrade to a paid systeme.io plan. We've tried to keep this comparison fair, and every number below comes from the vendors' own pages.

Where systeme.io wins

  • Cost to start. You can build and run a real funnel for $0. The cheapest ClickFunnels plan costs $97 a month, or $1,164 a year at the monthly rate.
  • Email volume. Every systeme.io plan includes unlimited email sends. ClickFunnels' Launch plan caps sends at 50,000 a month.
  • Low-risk testing. If you aren't sure your idea will sell, testing it on a free plan costs you time, not money.

Where ClickFunnels wins

  • Contacts on the entry plan. Launch includes 10,000 contacts, double systeme.io's Startup plan and five times its free plan. If you already have a big list, that matters.
  • Custom domains and workspaces. Launch includes five custom domains. Higher plans add workspaces for running several brands or client accounts.
  • Brand and ecosystem. ClickFunnels is one of the best-known funnel builders, so it's easy to find tutorials, templates and freelancers who already know it.

A simple way to decide

Choose systeme.io if:

  • You're starting from zero or have a small list
  • You want to test an offer before spending money
  • You send a lot of email relative to your list size
  • Keeping software costs low is a priority

Choose ClickFunnels if:

  • You already have more than 5,000 contacts and a proven offer
  • You need several domains or separate workspaces
  • You're hiring help and want a platform many freelancers already know
  • Spending $97 or more a month is small next to your revenue

What switching later looks like

You don't have to pick forever. Both platforms let you export your contacts. Many people start on a free plan, prove that the offer sells, and move only if they hit a limit that costs them money. Our guide to moving your setup without breaking things covers the steps.

The bottom line

For most beginners, the deciding question is whether $97 a month is worth it before you've made your first sale. If it isn't, start on systeme.io's free plan, then compare again once you've outgrown it.
