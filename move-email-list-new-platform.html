How to Move Your Email List to a New Platform Without Losing Subscribers

A step-by-step migration checklist: export, clean, set up authentication, import in batches, resume sequences, and warm up your sending so emails keep reaching the inbox.

October 25, 2026 · 3 min read

Disclosure: BizMasterPro is reader-supported. Some links are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission if you sign up for a paid plan, at no extra cost to you.

Switching email platforms can save money or add features, but a rushed move can cost you subscribers through lost data, duplicate emails or a sudden drop into spam folders. The fix is a careful, step-by-step migration. This checklist works for most platforms, with specifics from systeme.io's migration guides as an example.

Before you start

Write down everything your current platform does for you:

  • Lists, tags and segments
  • Automations and email sequences, including which email each subscriber is on
  • Opt-in forms embedded on your website or landing pages
  • Integrations, such as checkout, webinar or course tools

Don't cancel your old account yet. You'll need it for exports and as a backup.

Step 1: Export your contacts

Export your full list as a CSV file. Include every field you can: email, first name, tags, sign-up date and subscription status. Save an untouched copy as a backup before you change anything (systeme.io).

Step 2: Clean the list

Cleaning protects your sender reputation on the new platform:

  • Remove duplicates and obviously invalid addresses.
  • Separate active from inactive contacts. systeme.io suggests focusing on people who opened your emails in the last 30, 60 or 90 days (systeme.io).
  • Keep unsubscribes unsubscribed. Never reactivate people who opted out. Some platforms let you import them with an "unsubscribed" status so they're never emailed again (systeme.io).
  • Keep proof of consent. Platforms may ask for it, and you should only import people who agreed to hear from you (systeme.io).
  • Use UTF-8 encoding so names with accents don't turn into garbled characters (systeme.io).

Step 3: Set up the new platform first

Before importing anyone:

  1. Recreate your tags and segments with the same names.
  2. Rebuild your email sequences.
  3. Set up email authentication. Add the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records for your sending domain (systeme.io). Our deliverability guide explains each one.
  4. Add your postal address and sender details, which CAN-SPAM requires in every marketing email.

Step 4: Import carefully

  • Map every column to the right field: email, first name, tags and any custom fields. Mismapped columns are one of the most common causes of lost data (systeme.io).
  • Import in batches if you have thousands of contacts, starting with your most engaged subscribers.
  • Check the import report for errors after each batch.

Keep sequences in sync

If subscribers are partway through a sequence, you don't want them to start over. Some platforms let you map which email each person last received. systeme.io, for example, lets you map the step number and date of the last email sent, so the sequence picks up where it left off (systeme.io).

Watch for automatic emails

Adding contacts to a course or some automations can trigger emails immediately. systeme.io notes that adding someone to a course sends an access email right away, so stagger course imports (systeme.io).

Step 5: Warm up your sending

Inbox providers notice when a new sender suddenly emails thousands of people. Instead:

  1. Wait a day or two after importing before a big send (systeme.io).
  2. Send your first emails to your most engaged segment.
  3. Increase volume gradually over the following weeks.
  4. Send something genuinely useful first, not a hard sales pitch.

Your first email can also tell subscribers you've switched tools and ask them to reply or add your address to their contacts, which helps future emails reach the inbox.

Step 6: Switch over your forms

Replace every opt-in form and integration with the new platform's version. Test each one by signing up yourself and confirming you receive the welcome email.

Step 7: Close the old account last

Once forms are switched, sequences are running and a few sends have gone well, export one final copy from the old platform and then cancel.

Migration checklist

Step Done when...
Export You have a full CSV and an untouched backup
Clean Duplicates, invalid addresses and unsubscribes are handled
Set up Tags, sequences, authentication and footer are ready
Import Every batch's report shows no errors
Warm up Engaged segments get the first sends
Switch forms Every form sends new subscribers to the new platform
Close old account Final backup saved
