systeme.io Free Plan: What You Actually Get (and Where It Stops)

A plain-English look at systeme.io's free plan in 2026: the real limits, what's unlimited, and the point where upgrading makes sense.

October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

"Free" software usually means a trial, a watermark or a feature list with the useful parts missing. systeme.io's free plan is unusual because it never expires and doesn't ask for a credit card, according to its pricing page. Here's what the plan includes as of October 2026, where the limits are, and how to tell when you've outgrown it.

Source: systeme.io pricing. Plans change, so check the page before you plan around a specific limit.

What the limits mean in practice

2,000 contacts. For a new business, this usually lasts months. A contact is anyone on your list, so clean out people who never open your emails. That keeps you under the cap longer and helps your emails reach inboxes.

3 funnels. Most small businesses need fewer than they think. A practical setup is one opt-in funnel for your free resource, one sales funnel for your main product, and one spare for a webinar, a launch or testing a new offer.

1 course. Enough to launch and validate a first course. If you plan to sell several courses, this is the limit you'll feel.

Unlimited email. This is the most generous part of the plan. Many standalone email tools charge by list size or send volume, so unlimited sends on a free plan covers a real cost.

When to stay free and when to upgrade

Stay on the free plan if:

  • Your list is under about 1,500 contacts and growing slowly
  • Three funnels cover what you're running
  • You're selling one course or no course yet

Consider upgrading when:

  • You're close to 2,000 contacts after cleaning your list
  • You need a fourth funnel for something that will earn money
  • You're ready to sell a second course
  • The paid plan costs less than the tools it replaces, or your list already earns more than the plan costs

A simple free-plan setup

  1. Funnel 1: lead magnet. An opt-in page and a thank-you page that delivers a checklist or guide.
  2. Email campaign. Five to ten welcome emails that start automatically when someone joins.
  3. Funnel 2: offer. A sales page and order form for your product, service or course.
  4. Course. Your first course, if you have one, with students added automatically after purchase.
  5. Blog. A few helpful articles that point readers to your lead magnet.

The bottom line

For a business that's just starting, the free plan is usable on its own, not a teaser. The 2,000-contact cap and the 3-funnel limit are the two to watch. Build on it until one of them gets in the way of making money, then upgrade. You can open a free account here.
