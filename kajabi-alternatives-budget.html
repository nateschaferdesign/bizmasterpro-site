Kajabi Alternatives for Creators on a Budget (2026 Pricing)

Kajabi starts at $179 a month. Here's how systeme.io, Teachable and Thinkific compare on price, limits and transaction fees, using each company's pricing page.

October 23, 2026 · 3 min read

Disclosure: BizMasterPro is reader-supported. Some links are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission if you sign up for a paid plan, at no extra cost to you.

Kajabi is a polished all-in-one platform for courses, coaching and memberships, but its pricing is built for established businesses. If you're just starting, or your course hasn't made its first sale yet, there are cheaper ways to get the same core tools. Here's how four platforms compare, using figures from each company's pricing page as of October 2026. Prices change, so check before you sign up.

Kajabi's pricing, for reference

Kajabi's plans start at $179/month for Basic, with Growth at $249/month and Pro at $499/month on monthly billing, and lower rates if you pay annually (Kajabi). Basic allows 5 products. The pricing page doesn't list a free plan.

For a creator who isn't earning yet, that's more than $2,000 a year before the first sale.

systeme.io: lowest cost to start

systeme.io's free plan includes 1 course, up to 500 students, 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, unlimited emails and 0% transaction fees (systeme.io). Paid plans are $17/month (5 courses), $47/month (20 courses) and $97/month (unlimited).

Best for: Beginners who want courses, email marketing and sales funnels in one place without a monthly bill.

Trade-off: The design tools are simpler than Kajabi's, and the free plan limits you to one course.

Teachable: course-focused, with a fee on the starter plan

Teachable's Starter plan is $39/month, or $29/month billed annually, for 5 products, with a 7.5% transaction fee on sales (Teachable). The fee drops to 0% on Builder ($89/month) and higher plans when you use Teachable Payments, though standard processing fees still apply. Teachable says it doesn't offer a free plan.

Best for: Creators who mainly sell courses and digital downloads and want a dedicated course platform.

Trade-off: On Starter, 7.5% of every sale goes to Teachable, which adds up quickly as you grow.

Thinkific: unlimited courses, higher starting price

Thinkific's Start plan is $109/month, or $82/month billed annually, and includes unlimited published courses (Thinkific). It offers a 30-day free trial instead of a free plan. Thinkific Payments processing is 2.9%.

Best for: Educators planning several courses who want a strong learning experience.

Trade-off: It costs much more than systeme.io or Teachable to start, and email marketing tools are more limited than an all-in-one platform's.

How to choose

  1. Haven't made a sale yet? Start on a free plan and upgrade when revenue covers the cost.
  2. Selling one course plus emails and funnels? An all-in-one platform with a free tier avoids paying for separate tools.
  3. Planning many courses right away? Compare per-course limits; unlimited plans may be worth it.
  4. Expecting lots of sales? Look closely at transaction fees. A percentage fee can cost more than a higher monthly price.

Quick fee math

On $1,000 a month in course sales, a 7.5% transaction fee costs $75 a month in fees, before payment processing. Platforms with 0% transaction fees still charge standard card processing, so compare both numbers.

Bottom line

Kajabi is a strong platform, but you don't need it to launch. A free plan can host your first course, collect emails and run your sales page while you test the idea.
