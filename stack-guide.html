The idea

Most small online businesses pay for five or six separate tools that do overlapping jobs. Each one is cheap on its own, and together they often cost more than the business earns in its first year. This guide shows how to run the same setup from one all-in-one platform, systeme.io, starting on its free plan.

Step 1: Set up your account (10 minutes)

  1. Open a free account. No card is needed for the free plan.
  2. Add your sending details: a business name and a physical mailing address. US email law requires the address in every marketing email.
  3. Connect your own domain if you have one. It helps your emails reach the inbox.

Step 2: Build your first opt-in funnel (20 minutes)

  1. Create a new funnel and choose the "Build audience" type.
  2. Page 1 is your opt-in page. Write one headline, three bullets and a form that asks only for a first name and email.
  3. Page 2 is your thank-you page. Deliver the freebie you promised and add one next step.
  4. Add a tag such as lead_magnet when someone signs up. Tags let you send the right emails later.

Step 3: Write a short welcome sequence (30 minutes)

Five emails over ten days is enough to start:

  1. Day 0: Deliver what you promised and tell people what's coming.
  2. Day 1: Tell a short story about the problem you solve.
  3. Day 3: Teach one useful thing in a few steps.
  4. Day 6: Answer the most common objection.
  5. Day 10: Invite them to the next step, such as a product, a call or your newsletter.

Every email needs an unsubscribe link and your mailing address. The platform adds the unsubscribe link for you.

Step 4: Add the one automation that does follow-up for you

Create a rule: when the tag lead_magnet is added, subscribe the contact to your welcome campaign. That single rule turns your funnel into a system that runs while you sleep.

Then add two more as you grow:

  • When someone clicks a product link but doesn't buy, add a tag and send a short "need help?" email two days later.
  • When someone hasn't opened an email in 30 days, send one re-engagement email, then remove them if they still don't open. This protects your deliverability.

Step 5: Know when to upgrade

Stay on the free plan until you hit a real limit, usually the contact cap or the number of funnels. Upgrade when the paid plan costs less than the tools it replaces, or when your list is earning more than the plan costs. Don't upgrade just because a feature looks nice.

Common mistakes

  • Asking for too much on the opt-in form. Name and email only.
  • Sending from a free email address instead of your own domain.
  • Writing long, salesy emails. Short and useful wins.
  • Never cleaning the list. Inactive contacts hurt delivery for everyone else.
