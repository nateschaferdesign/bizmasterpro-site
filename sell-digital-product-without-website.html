How to Sell Your First Digital Product Without a Website

A step-by-step way to sell a PDF, template or mini-course using just a link in your bio, a checkout page and automatic delivery. No website required.

October 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Disclosure: BizMasterPro is reader-supported. Some links are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission if you sign up for a paid plan, at no extra cost to you.

You don't need a full website to sell your first digital product. A single link in your social bio, a checkout page and automatic delivery are enough to take payments and send the product while you sleep. Here's how to set that up, using systeme.io as the example because its free plan includes the pieces you need.

What you need

  • A product. A PDF guide, a template, a checklist, a short course or a set of audio files.
  • A payment processor. systeme.io connects to Stripe and PayPal (systeme.io).
  • A checkout page. Either a creator store or a simple two-page funnel.
  • Automatic delivery. An email or members-area access that's sent the moment someone pays.

Step 1: Pick one small product people already want

Start with something narrow that solves one problem quickly. Good first products include a template people would otherwise build themselves, a checklist for a task they do often, or a short video course on one skill. Before you build it, check demand by looking at what already sells in your niche and asking your audience what they'd pay for (systeme.io).

Step 2: Make it, then test it with a few people

Create a first version and give it to two or three people for feedback before you sell it widely. It's much cheaper to fix problems now. Done and useful beats perfect and unreleased.

Step 3: Choose your storefront

You have two simple options, and neither needs a website.

Option Best for How it works
Creator store Selling from a social bio link One shareable page lists your products, and each has its own checkout (systeme.io)
Two-page funnel One focused product A sales page and an order form, with a thank-you page after purchase (systeme.io Help)

If you sell mostly through Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, start with the creator store. If you'll send traffic from emails or articles to one product, use a funnel.

Step 4: Set up the checkout

In systeme.io, an order form asks you to choose an offer type. Choose Digital product, then add the resource the buyer gets after paying: a course, a course bundle, a community or a tag (systeme.io Help). Add a price plan and connect your payment method.

Step 5: Deliver it automatically

For a PDF or template, systeme.io's help center describes two easy delivery methods (systeme.io Help):

  1. Email attachment. Create an automation rule with the trigger "New sale" and the action "Send email," then attach the file.
  2. Download link. Send a link to the file instead. This keeps emails small, which helps them reach the inbox.

For a course, buyers get access to it automatically after a successful payment.

Step 6: Price it on the outcome

Price your product on the result it gives the buyer, not on the hours it took you to make. Underpricing is the most common mistake (systeme.io). Start with a price you can defend, then test what your audience will pay.

Step 7: Write one clear sales page

Keep it to five parts:

  1. The problem you solve
  2. Exactly what the buyer gets
  3. Proof it works, such as your own results or honest feedback from testers
  4. The price
  5. One clear buy button

Step 8: Put the link everywhere

Copy your store or funnel link into your social bios, the end of your videos, your email signature and your welcome emails. Every post should point somewhere you can sell.

Before you go live

  • Buy your own product once to test the checkout and delivery.
  • Check the confirmation email on your phone.
  • Make sure your refund policy is clear on the checkout page.
  • Add your business name and contact details.
